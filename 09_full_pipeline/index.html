Why the winner won: the five score terms

Each bar is one term of the winner's score, normalised within this candidate set (0 is the median candidate; negative is better than the median). density = columns per floor area; span demand = span² times tributary width, the load-free bending index; irregularity = spread of the bay sizes; off-wall = columns landing outside walls; repetition = distinct bay sizes. The weights on these five terms are the score function's only free parameters.
